FAQs

Frequently asked
questions.

What services does AirBrick Infra provide for offices in Gurgaon?

A complete design-and-build solution: AI-driven space planning, 3D visualisation, procurement and turnkey execution for modern commercial offices.

Why choose AirBrick over traditional interior design companies?

Through technology we bring transparency, predictability and speed — AI layouts, digital BOQs and real-time project tracking — which reduces uncertainty and improves delivery quality.

Can I see how my office will look before construction starts?

Yes. We provide 3D renders and virtual walkthroughs so you can preview and approve the design before anything is built.

How does AI help in designing office layouts?

Our AI uses design rules, space-utilisation data and best-practice layouts to generate efficient plans — faster revisions, optimal use of space and cost-effective designs.

Which parts of Gurgaon do you work in?

We operate across major business hubs in Gurgaon such as Cyber City, Golf Course Road, Sohna Road and more.

Can you handle construction, MEP and furniture installation too?

Yes — structural work, electricals, HVAC, lighting, networking and furniture procurement & installation through our trusted vendor network.

Will my office need to shut down during renovation or build?

Not necessarily. We plan phased work so parts of your office can stay operational while construction is on, minimising downtime.

How do you ensure quality and accountability during execution?

A tech-enabled QC process tracks vendor performance and quality checks digitally, with a single project lead overseeing everything.
